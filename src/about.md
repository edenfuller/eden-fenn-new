**Eden Fenn** writes strange stories about devotion and desire. She lives in Baltimore.

Eden's current project is **_The Candle at Both Ends_**, an occult horror novel about a toxic nonprofit wielding magic that consumes its employees' life essence and a lesbian who's in love with her boss.

Eden's short stories have appeared
in _Nature Futures_, _Daily Science Fiction_, and _Asimov's Science Fiction Magazine_.
